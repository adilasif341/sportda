When Was the NFL Founded? Complete History of the National Football League

Published: January 2026 | Reading Time: 3 minutes

The National Football League (NFL) was founded on September 17, 1920, in Canton, Ohio, when representatives from ten professional football teams gathered to form the American Professional Football Association (APFA). This historic meeting marked the beginning of what would become the most successful professional sports league in the United States. The league officially changed its name to the National Football League in 1922, establishing the foundation for over a century of professional football excellence. For a detailed exploration of the NFL's origins and early years, visit our comprehensive guide on when the NFL was established.

The founding teams included franchises from cities like Akron, Canton, Cleveland, and Dayton in Ohio, along with teams from Illinois, Indiana, New York, and Pennsylvania. The league's first president was Jim Thorpe, one of the greatest athletes of the early 20th century, who helped legitimize professional football at a time when the sport was still developing. The early years were marked by instability, with teams frequently folding or relocating, but the league gradually established itself as the premier professional football organization in America.

From its humble beginnings with just ten teams, the NFL has grown into a 32-team league that generates billions of dollars in revenue annually and captivates millions of fans worldwide. The league's evolution from a regional organization to a national powerhouse reflects the growth of American football itself, transforming from a niche sport into a cultural phenomenon that dominates American sports culture. Understanding the NFL's founding provides valuable context for appreciating how far the league and the sport have come over the past century.
